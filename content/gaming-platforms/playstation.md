# PlayStation

PlayStation is one of the biggest names in console gaming. Sony has released several generations of PlayStation systems, and each generation has improved the graphics, speed, storage, and online features.

## Games

One thing PlayStation is known for is its exclusive games. Some games become closely connected with the PlayStation brand.

[[popular-games/god-of-war|God of War]] is one example. The series became well known through PlayStation systems and is one of the games people often think about when talking about PlayStation.

### Console Gaming

PlayStation is simpler than building a gaming PC because the hardware is already chosen for the player. You buy the console, connect it to a TV, and start playing.

Players can also use online features to play with friends, download games, and access other entertainment.

PlayStation is part of the larger history of [[gaming-history/home-consoles|home consoles]]. Consoles made gaming easier for people who did not want to use an arcade machine or computer.
