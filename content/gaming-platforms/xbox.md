# Xbox

Xbox is Microsoft's gaming console brand. It entered the console market after companies like Nintendo, Sega, and Sony were already well known.

## Xbox and Halo

One of the games most closely connected with Xbox is [[popular-games/halo|Halo]]. Halo helped make the original Xbox stand out because it gave players a major first-person shooter that was strongly connected to the console.

Xbox has continued to focus a lot on online gaming. Xbox Live helped make playing console games online much more common.

### Modern Xbox Gaming

Xbox players today can buy games digitally, play online, use subscription services, and play certain games across Xbox and PC.

The Xbox controller has also become common outside of the console itself. Many PC players use Xbox-style [[gaming-hardware/controllers|controllers]].

I think Xbox is important because it helped push online console gaming forward. It also created more competition between console companies, especially between Xbox and [[gaming-platforms/playstation|PlayStation]].
