# PC Gaming

PC gaming means playing video games on a computer instead of a console. One of the biggest differences with PC gaming is that people can choose and upgrade different parts of their computer.

## Customization

A gaming PC can be changed depending on what the player wants. Someone might upgrade their [[gaming-hardware/graphics-cards|graphics card]], add more storage, or buy a better monitor.

PC players also have different choices for controls. Some use a keyboard and mouse while others use a [[gaming-hardware/controllers|controller]].

### Advantages

- Hardware can be upgraded
- Large game libraries
- Different control options
- Graphics settings can be changed
- Many online games are available

One downside is that PC gaming can get expensive, especially when someone wants high-end parts. It can also be more confusing for a new player compared to buying a console and simply turning it on.

I think the main reason people like PC gaming is the amount of control they have over their setup and how they want their games to run.
