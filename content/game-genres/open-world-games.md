# Open-World Games

Open-world games give players a large area to explore instead of forcing them to follow one path the whole time. The player can normally decide where they want to go and what they want to do.

## Freedom to Explore

One of the biggest reasons people like open-world games is the freedom. Sometimes you can spend hours doing side activities without even touching the main story.

[[popular-games/grand-theft-auto-v|Grand Theft Auto V]] is a good example. Players can complete missions, drive around the city, explore, buy things, and do other activities. Games like [[popular-games/minecraft|Minecraft]] also give players a lot of freedom, even though Minecraft works differently.

### Why It Works

Open-world games can make the player feel like they are actually part of the world. There is usually more to discover than in a smaller game.

That freedom is probably the biggest thing that separates open-world games from more linear games.
