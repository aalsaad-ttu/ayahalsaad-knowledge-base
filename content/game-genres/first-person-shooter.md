# First-Person Shooters

First-person shooters, usually called FPS games, are games where you see the action from the point of view of the character you are controlling. Most of the time the main focus is using weapons and reacting quickly to enemies.

## Why They Are Popular

FPS games can be very competitive, especially online. Players usually need good aim, fast reactions, and knowledge of the maps. Some people like playing casually while others take the games much more seriously.

Two popular examples are [[popular-games/call-of-duty|Call of Duty]] and [[popular-games/halo|Halo]]. Both are shooters, but they feel very different. Call of Duty usually tries to feel more realistic, while Halo has more science-fiction weapons and environments.

### Common Features

- Guns and other weapons
- Multiplayer matches
- Different maps
- Teams or solo modes
- Ranking systems

I think FPS games stay popular because the gameplay is easy to understand, but getting really good at them can take a long time.
