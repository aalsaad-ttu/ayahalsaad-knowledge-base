# Grand Theft Auto V

Grand Theft Auto V is a game that stands out because of how much there is to do outside of the main story. The game takes place in a large open world, and players can drive around, explore different areas, complete missions, and interact with the world in different ways. I think the amount of freedom the game gives players is one of the main reasons it has stayed popular for so many years.

## The Open World

One of my favorite things about GTA V is that you do not always have to focus on completing missions. Sometimes simply driving around Los Santos and exploring can be entertaining. The game includes cars, motorcycles, planes, stores, different neighborhoods, and many other things that make the world feel active.

### GTA Online

GTA Online expanded the game even further by allowing players to play in the same world with other people. Players can complete missions, buy vehicles and properties, and participate in different activities.

> Open-world games are interesting to me because they give players more control over what they want to do instead of always forcing them to follow one path.

In that way, GTA V has something in common with [[popular-games/minecraft|Minecraft]]. Both allow players to explore at their own pace, even though their worlds and gameplay are completely different.

GTA V also has a stronger focus on story and characters, which connects it somewhat to [[popular-games/god-of-war|God of War]].
