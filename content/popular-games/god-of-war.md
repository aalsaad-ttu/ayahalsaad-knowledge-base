# God of War

God of War is different from most of the other games in this section because it is much more focused on story and single-player gameplay. The series follows Kratos, a warrior who has faced gods and other powerful enemies throughout his life. What interests me about God of War is that the newer games are not only about fighting. They also focus heavily on Kratos as a character and his relationship with his son, Atreus.

## Combat and Story

The combat is one of the biggest parts of the game. Kratos uses weapons such as the Leviathan Axe, and battles can involve regular enemies, monsters, and very powerful bosses. The fighting feels heavy and powerful, which matches the type of character Kratos is.

### What Makes It Different

God of War stands out because the story is just as important as the action. The game spends a lot of time developing its characters instead of only moving from one battle to another. That makes it feel different from multiplayer-focused games like [[popular-games/call-of-duty|Call of Duty]] and [[popular-games/halo|Halo]].

I like that difference because I do not always want the same type of gaming experience. Sometimes competitive multiplayer is fun, but other times I would rather play something that has a strong story and feels more like an adventure.

God of War shows how a game can combine combat, exploration, characters, and storytelling into one experience.
