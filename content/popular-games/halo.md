# Halo

Halo is another game series that stands out to me because it has a completely different style from most military shooters. Instead of focusing on realistic weapons and modern warfare, Halo takes place in a science-fiction universe with futuristic weapons, vehicles, aliens, and large environments. That setting is one of the biggest reasons the game feels unique. My husband and I also have completed all the campaigns together. 

## Gameplay

Halo gives players different ways to experience the game. The campaigns focus more on the story, while multiplayer lets players compete against each other. I especially like how the game includes vehicles and larger maps because it changes the way matches are played. Sometimes you can be fighting another player normally, while other times someone comes driving across the map in a vehicle.

### Halo Compared to Other Games

The game has similarities to [[popular-games/call-of-duty|Call of Duty]] because both are first-person shooters and have popular multiplayer modes. At the same time, they do not really feel the same when you play them. Halo usually takes more damage before a player is defeated, which can make fights last longer.

I also think Halo is different from a game like [[popular-games/god-of-war|God of War]] because Halo focuses heavily on shooting and multiplayer, while God of War is much more focused on its story and characters. That difference shows how broad gaming can be even when games are all considered action games.
