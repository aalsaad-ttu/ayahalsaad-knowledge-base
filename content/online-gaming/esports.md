# Esports

Esports are organized video game competitions. Players or teams compete against each other, sometimes for prize money.

## Competitive Gaming

Not every multiplayer game becomes an esport. Games usually need a strong competitive community and rules that work well for tournaments.

Esports can include shooters, strategy games, fighting games, and sports games.

### More Than Just Playing

Professional players spend a lot of time practicing. Teams may have coaches, schedules, and strategies just like traditional sports teams.

Fans can watch tournaments online or sometimes attend events in person.

Esports grew partly because of the growth of [[online-gaming/multiplayer-gaming|multiplayer gaming]] and streaming.

Large [[online-gaming/gaming-communities|gaming communities]] also help esports grow because players and fans can discuss matches and follow teams.

I think esports show how much gaming has changed. Video games started mainly as entertainment, but now some people compete professionally while millions of others watch them play.
