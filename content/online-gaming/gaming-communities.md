# Online Gaming Communities

Gaming communities are groups of people who connect because they enjoy the same games or gaming in general.

## Where Communities Form

Gaming communities can form inside games or on places like forums, social media, Discord servers, and streaming sites.

Some communities focus on one game while others talk about gaming as a whole.

### Good and Bad Sides

Gaming communities can be useful because people share tips, help new players, and find others to play with.

They can also have problems with arguing, cheating, or toxic behavior.

Communities are especially important in [[online-gaming/multiplayer-gaming|multiplayer games]] because players often need teammates or other people to compete against.

They also help support [[online-gaming/esports|esports]] by giving fans a place to follow teams, players, and tournaments.

I think gaming communities are one of the main reasons some games stay popular for years.
