# Multiplayer Gaming

Multiplayer gaming means more than one person can play the same game together or against each other.

## Different Types

Multiplayer can happen locally, where people are in the same room, or online through the internet.

Online multiplayer is now one of the biggest parts of gaming.

Games like [[popular-games/call-of-duty|Call of Duty]] and [[popular-games/halo|Halo]] are well known for letting players compete online.

### Why People Like It

- Playing with friends
- Competing against other people
- Teamwork
- Meeting new players
- Ranking up

Multiplayer games can feel different every time because human players do not always behave the same way computer-controlled characters do.

They can also create friendships and larger [[online-gaming/gaming-communities|gaming communities]].

Competitive multiplayer has even grown into [[online-gaming/esports|esports]], where skilled players compete in organized events.

I think multiplayer is one of the biggest reasons gaming has become so social. Gaming is no longer always something someone does alone.
