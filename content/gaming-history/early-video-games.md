# Early Video Games

Video games started out much simpler than the games we have today. Early games had basic graphics, simple controls, and limited sound.

## Arcades

Arcade games became a major part of early gaming. People would go to arcades and pay to play games on large machines.

Games such as Pong and Space Invaders became well known and helped video games become more popular.

### Simple but Important

Early games did not have huge open worlds or realistic graphics, but they helped create many ideas that are still used today.

Players were already competing for high scores and playing against each other.

Over time, technology improved and gaming moved from arcades into people's homes. This eventually led to the growth of [[gaming-history/home-consoles|home consoles]].

I think it is interesting to compare early games to modern games like [[popular-games/grand-theft-auto-v|Grand Theft Auto V]]. The difference shows how quickly gaming technology has changed in only a few decades.
