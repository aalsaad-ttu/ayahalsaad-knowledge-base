# History of Online Gaming

Online gaming changed the way people play video games. Instead of only playing with someone in the same room, players could connect with people in other places.

## Early Online Play

Early online gaming was much more limited because internet speeds were slower. As internet connections improved, online multiplayer became much easier.

PC games were some of the first places where large online gaming communities became common.

### Console Online Gaming

Console companies later made online play a major feature too. Services on Xbox and PlayStation made it easy to add friends and join matches.

Games like [[popular-games/call-of-duty|Call of Duty]] and [[popular-games/halo|Halo]] became closely connected with online multiplayer.

Online gaming eventually grew into things like [[online-gaming/esports|esports]] and large [[online-gaming/gaming-communities|gaming communities]].

Today it is normal for a game to have online features, but that was not always the case. The growth of online gaming completely changed gaming from something mostly local into something much more connected.
