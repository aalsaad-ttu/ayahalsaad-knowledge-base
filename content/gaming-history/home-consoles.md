# Rise of Home Consoles

Home consoles changed gaming because people no longer had to go to an arcade to play video games. They could play from their own living room.

## Early Consoles

As technology improved, more companies started making systems that connected directly to a television.

Over time, companies like Nintendo, Sony, and Microsoft became major names in the console market.

### Modern Consoles

Today, systems like [[gaming-platforms/playstation|PlayStation]] and [[gaming-platforms/xbox|Xbox]] can do much more than just play games. Players can download games, watch videos, use apps, and play online.

Consoles also made gaming easier for many people because they did not need to understand computer hardware.

Compared with [[gaming-platforms/pc-gaming|PC gaming]], consoles usually give players fewer hardware choices, but they are also simpler to set up.

Home consoles helped make gaming a normal form of entertainment for millions of people and played a major role in how the gaming industry grew.
