# Controllers and Input Devices

Controllers and input devices are how players actually control a game. The two most common choices are a controller or a keyboard and mouse.

## Controllers

Controllers are common on consoles like [[gaming-platforms/xbox|Xbox]] and [[gaming-platforms/playstation|PlayStation]]. They usually include buttons, triggers, and two analog sticks.

Controllers can be more comfortable for games involving driving, sports, or third-person movement.

## Keyboard and Mouse

Keyboard and mouse controls are especially common in [[gaming-platforms/pc-gaming|PC gaming]]. A mouse can allow very fast and accurate aiming, which is useful in shooters.

### Personal Preference

Neither option is automatically better for every game. Some players switch depending on what they are playing.

For example, someone may use a keyboard and mouse for a shooter but use a controller for a racing game.

The type of input device can change how a game feels. Even though it seems like a small part of gaming, it has a major effect on how players interact with the game.
