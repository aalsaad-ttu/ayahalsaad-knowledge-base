# Graphics Cards

A graphics card is one of the most important parts of a gaming computer. Its job is to process the graphics that appear on the screen.

## Why It Matters

A stronger graphics card can usually run games at higher settings and better frame rates. It can also help with higher screen resolutions.

This is especially important for [[gaming-platforms/pc-gaming|PC gaming]] because different computers can have very different graphics cards.

### Performance

Some of the things affected by a graphics card include:

- Resolution
- Frame rate
- Texture quality
- Lighting
- Shadows
- Visual effects

Players do not always need the most expensive graphics card. It really depends on what games they play and what kind of [[gaming-hardware/gaming-monitors|monitor]] they use.

For example, someone using a basic monitor may not need the same hardware as someone trying to play at a very high resolution and frame rate.

Graphics cards can be confusing at first because there are so many different models, but they are a major part of gaming PC performance.
